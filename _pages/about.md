I am a Senior AI Research Scientist and Tech Lead in the Voice team at NAVER Cloud. My research focuses on **Speech-Language & Multimodal AI** and **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, with the goal of building AI systems that understand, reason, and interact across speech, language, and context.

I develop language and speech technologies for real-world AI products, spanning pronunciation modeling, phrase break prediction, data generation, and model evaluation. Recently, I have been exploring how LLMs can extend these systems through multimodal reasoning, tool use, and scalable agentic applications. My work includes publications at [EMNLP](https://2026.emnlp.org/) and [INTERSPEECH](https://interspeech2026.org/).

I connect research and engineering through technical leadership, mentoring, and production deployment across [NAVER](https://www.naver.com/)’s services. Since April 2026, I have also served as an AI Technical Mentor at [AI·SW Maestro](https://www.swmaestro.ai/sw/main/main.do), guiding two teams developing production-grade AI systems.

If you are interested in collaboration or would like to get in touch, please feel free to [contact me](/contacts/).
